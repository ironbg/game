## Placeholder rules
- `{stage}` names carry the full article, so inside a sentence always quote them: „{stage}“.
- `{hero}` names are bare nouns (Рицар, Скалд); use "докато играеш като {hero}" instead of "с {hero}" (avoids с/със and case).
- Counts that may be 1: prefer "Label: {n}" over agreeing nouns ("Победи босове: {n}").
- Units take a space: "{v} сек", "{t} мин", "{max} ч".
